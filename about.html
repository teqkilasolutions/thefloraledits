The studio

Slow clay, fast delivery

The Floral Edit makes and sells vases for people who want flowers to look considered without a lot of effort. Tell us the room, we suggest the vessel.

Our approach

Vessel first, flowers second

Most bouquets look better in a better vase. So we start with the vessel: its shape, its glaze, the height it needs. Then we pair it with flowers that suit it, and sell the two together or apart.

Studio pieces are made in small batches. Theme pots take their colours and patterns from films, series and festivals, using original designs only.

From order to doorstep

How a vase reaches you

Step one

You choose

Pick a vase alone, with our signature flowers, or with flowers you choose.

Step two

We arrange

Fresh flowers are sourced for your date and conditioned before styling. Availability decides the timing.

Step three

We pack

The vase sits in a fitted box with a water-safe liner and a handwritten card.

Step four

Next day for common flowers, 2 days for sourced ones and up to 6 days for rare stems.

Care and questions

Good to know

Short answers to what people ask us most. For anything else, write to the studio below.

How do I care for a glazed vase?

Wash by hand with warm water and mild soap. Dry upright. Avoid dishwashers and sudden temperature changes.

How long do arranged flowers last?

Fresh arrangements typically last five to seven days with fresh water every second day. Dried and preserved pieces last a year or more away from direct sun.

Can I order a vase without flowers?

Yes. Every vase has a vase-only option, packed empty and ready for your own flowers.

Do you style events?

Yes. Use the form below for weddings, pujas, mehendis, dinners and office events. We usually reply within a day.

What if my vase arrives damaged?

Message us within 24 hours with a photo and we will replace it.
